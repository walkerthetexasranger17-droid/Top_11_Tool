# Top Eleven Tool v0.4.18

## Scanner v12 — native resolution for every field

- Removed whole-screenshot resizing entirely and moved the scanner baseline to the native 2688×1216 Top Eleven layout.
- The uploaded image remains at its original resolution throughout recognition.
- Existing ROI coordinates are used only as proportional coordinates and are scaled to the native screenshot dimensions before cropping.
- Name, OVR, age, roles, totals, all skills, playstyle and coloured Special Ability slots now use source pixels.
- Compact playstyle references, deterministic level comparison, max-XP-arrow masking and coloured-only Special Ability references are unchanged.
